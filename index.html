FactMotionDaily Uploader

FactMotionDaily Uploader is a private desktop tool used to upload and manage original FactMotionDaily short-form educational videos to TikTok.

The application is intended for use by the owner of the FactMotionDaily account. It does not sell access to TikTok accounts, does not provide public social-media management services, and does not collect unnecessary personal information.

Purpose

The tool helps prepare and upload original short-form videos created for the FactMotionDaily channel. TikTok authentication is handled through TikTok's official authorization process.
