Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

FactMotionDaily Uploader is a private desktop application used by the FactMotionDaily channel owner to upload and manage original videos on TikTok.

Information We Access

When the user connects a TikTok account, the application may receive authentication tokens and basic account information that TikTok makes available through its official developer APIs. The application may also access video files, captions, and related metadata selected for upload.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to authenticate the connected TikTok account, upload user-selected video content, retrieve upload or publishing status, and prevent accidental duplicate uploads.

Storage

Authentication tokens and upload records may be stored locally on the user's own computer. FactMotionDaily does not operate a separate public database for this tool and does not sell personal information.

Sharing

Information is shared only as necessary with TikTok through TikTok's official APIs in order to perform the requested upload or publishing functions. We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

Data Retention

Local authentication and upload records are retained only as needed for the application to function. The user may delete locally stored application data at any time.

Security

Reasonable steps are taken to keep authentication credentials private. Users should not share client secrets, access tokens, or refresh tokens with others.

Your Choices

The user may disconnect TikTok authorization and stop using the application at any time. Access may also be revoked through TikTok account settings where available.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's functionality changes or if required by TikTok's platform policies.

Contact

For privacy questions regarding this application, contact the FactMotionDaily channel owner through the public contact method listed on the FactMotionDaily channel profile.
